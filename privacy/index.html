Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026
Developer: Lahbib Semlali

Photos and local analysis
Sweep accesses only the photo library items you authorize through Apple's permission controls. Limited access works with the items you choose. Media previews, resource fingerprints, look-alike comparisons, advisory face/image quality scoring, background removal and file-size measurements run on your device. Sweep does not identify people from faces. Sweep does not send your photos, videos, fingerprints, library identifiers or review decisions to our servers or RevenueCat.

Local storage
Sweep stores library metadata, fingerprints, review decisions, category selection drafts, preferences, lifetime trial-usage counters and cleanup history in its app storage. Image and video previews may be cached locally. These files may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings. Uninstalling removes app-local data from the device; it does not remove existing backups or cancel a subscription.

iCloud and Apple Photos
While displaying library thumbnails or opening a media preview, Apple may download media from your iCloud Photos library. Automatic analysis skips resources unavailable locally. Sweep's approved deletions use Apple Photos. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple synchronizes those deletions to your other devices. Apple's services and recovery rules apply.

Purchases and RevenueCat
Apple processes payments. Sweep uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore access and maintain subscription status. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, transaction or receipt information, and app/device information needed for its service. Purchase history supports app functionality and RevenueCat's dashboard analytics. Sweep does not request your name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifier for purchase verification. We do not link the anonymous purchase identifier to an account with us. RevenueCat's privacy information is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Updates and network services
Sweep uses Apple's services for Photos and purchases and RevenueCat for purchase verification. Remote JavaScript updates are disabled in this release. Network providers receive information inherent in a network request, such as the IP address. Personal media is not part of purchase verification requests. If you choose Share or export, the receiving app or location you select gets that media. We do not use advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, and do not sell personal information.

Support and retention
If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send. Please avoid including sensitive photos. We use support correspondence to resolve your request. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records under their own policies and legal obligations. We retain support correspondence while needed to resolve your request and related obligations.

Your controls
Change Photos access in iOS Settings at any time. Use Apple Settings to manage iCloud, backups and subscriptions. Contact us to request access to or deletion of support information we hold. Removing Sweep does not cancel an Apple subscription. Rights may vary by jurisdiction.

Changes and contact
We update this policy when our practices change. Questions or privacy requests: lahbibdev@gmail.com
