Using Sweep
Use Sweep only with media you are authorized to access. The app provides local review and cleanup tools. You retain ownership of your media. Apple's standard licensed application agreement also applies: apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/.

Your deletion decisions
You decide which items to queue, inspect and approve for deletion. Review undo reverses the last saved review choice before deletion; it cannot recover a PhotoKit deletion. Deleting a Live Photo removes both its still image and motion. Similarity suggestions are candidates, not proof that an item is unwanted. Review expressions, edits, Live Photos and other details before approving. PhotoKit and iCloud Photos perform authorized deletions. Recently Deleted normally retains removed media for 30 days, unless permanently deleted sooner. Recovery is limited and is not guaranteed. File-size estimates are not a guarantee of immediate device storage savings.

Free and Pro access
Free includes library indexing and browsing, manual inspection, 25 saved swipes, 25 confirmed cleanup items, 5 distinct completed photo-group choices and 1 successful background removal per installation. These allowances do not reset daily. Successful Keep, delete-queue and Vault swipes count; failed or skipped actions do not. Undo does not replenish swipes. Failed cutouts and cancelled deletions do not consume their respective allowances. Trial counters are stored locally and can be removed when app data is deleted; they are not shared across devices. Sweep Pro provides unlimited swipes, cleanup, group review and cutouts, full accessible-library photo analysis, quality guidance and Safe Picks batches. Pro activity does not consume the free allowances. If Pro expires, previously used free allowances remain used. Features depend on locally accessible media, permissions and supported iOS APIs. Background removal saves a new image and does not delete the original.

Purchases

Weekly and annual subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; renewal is charged within 24 hours before that period ends. Manage or cancel in App Store account settings. Cancelling stops future renewals and normally retains access through the paid period. Restore purchases using the same Apple account. Any trial, if offered, must be disclosed with its duration and subsequent price before purchase. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Media may be unavailable, edited, read-only or excluded by permissions. Detection is conservative but may miss matches or suggest unrelated images. Storage also changes through system activity and other apps. We cannot guarantee detection accuracy, recovery or a particular amount of reclaimed storage.
