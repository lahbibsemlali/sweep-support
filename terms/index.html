Your deletion decisions
You decide which items to queue, inspect and approve for deletion. Similarity suggestions are candidates, not proof that an item is unwanted. Review expressions, edits, Live Photos and other details before approving. PhotoKit and iCloud Photos perform authorized deletions. Recently Deleted normally retains removed media for 30 days, unless permanently deleted sooner. Recovery is limited and is not guaranteed. File-size estimates are not a guarantee of immediate device storage savings.

Free and Pro access
Manual photo review, screenshot review, large-video review and approved deletion are available without a daily deletion or swipe limit. Sweep Pro adds on-device exact-copy resource analysis and look-alike suggestions. Availability depends on locally accessible media and device permissions.
