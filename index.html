Sweep helps you review your photo library calmly, on your iPhone. Every deletion is your decision.

Sweep is in development. This is its privacy and support site; it does not announce App Store availability.

Need a hand?

Email lahbibdev@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, Sweep version and what happened. Please avoid sending sensitive photos.

Your library stays under your control

Manual review and approved deletion have no daily limit. Automatic analysis runs on your device. You can choose limited photo access and change it later in Settings.

Purchases and recovery

Restore Pro from Sweep Settings using the same Apple account. Manage weekly or annual renewal in App Store subscriptions. Uninstalling an app does not cancel a subscription.

For approved deletions, check Photos → Recently Deleted. Recovery is limited and is not guaranteed. iCloud Photos synchronizes deletion across devices, and storage may not be reclaimed immediately.
